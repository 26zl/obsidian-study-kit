# Weekly review

Once a week, look at what the logs say, which is often different from how the week felt. Lead with the numbers, then let the student interpret them.

Follow the vault rules in `AGENTS.md`. Speak the language set in `profile.md`.

## 1. The period

Review the ISO week that has just ended, or the current week if the student runs this on its last day. The note is `weekly/YYYY-Www.md` inside the daily notes folder, so `daily/weekly/2026-W47.md` in the kit, created from `templates/weekly-review.md`.

## 2. Collect

- The week's daily notes: the "Done" and "Slipped" sections.
- `courses/study-plan.md`: sessions ticked and unticked for the week.
- Entries in `progress/` logs dated within the week: how many attempts, the scores, and what recurs under "Misunderstood" and "Missed".
- Assignments due in the next 14 days and exams in the next 30.

## 3. Write the numbers

- Sessions planned and sessions done.
- Days with a recall session.
- Attempts and average score per course.
- Topics still scoring 0 or 1 after two or more attempts.

Report them as they are. Where there is no data, say there is none. Do not estimate.

## 4. Ask two questions

One at a time, and wait for each answer:

1. What worked this week that you want to keep?
2. What got in the way most?

Write the answers into the note in the student's own words.

## 5. Name the patterns

At most three, each backed by lines from the logs: "mixed up reliability and validity in three attempts", "no session on any day with a late shift". A pattern without evidence is a guess, so leave it out.

## 6. Set next week

- Three priorities.
- The changes to the plan that follow from the patterns.
- If more than a third of the planned sessions slipped, offer to rebuild the plan with `study-plan`.

Keep the tone accurate: no cheering and no scolding. The student can see the numbers. What helps is a next week that fits the life they have.
