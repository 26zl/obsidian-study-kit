## Your brain

Press Ctrl+G, or Cmd+G on a Mac, to open the graph. Every note is a dot and every link a line, coloured by kind:

| Colour | What |
|---|---|
| Gold | Course homes |
| Violet | Topics you must master |
| Cyan | Sources, lecture notes and source notes |
| Pink | Progress logs |
| Orange | Assignments and exam notes |
| Blue | Linked repos |
| Green | Areas of life |
| Grey | Daily notes |

The more you link, the more it looks like a brain. Say **brain map DEMO101** to see how a course's topics connect, as in [[DEMO101.canvas|the demo map]].
