# Learning how to learn

A sample course that ships with the kit so you can try the skills before adding your own courses. Its subject is the study techniques the kit is built on, so practising here also shows you why the skills work the way they do.

Try: "test me on DEMO101", "what is due for recall?" or "help me understand spacing".

When you are done with it, set `status` to `done` to take it off the dashboards, or delete the folder.

## Assessment

There is no real exam. The questions in [[question-bank]] are written the way a short-answer exam would ask them.

A practice course, so nothing here is graded.
