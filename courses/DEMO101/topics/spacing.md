# Spacing

## In my own words

Write this yourself after reading the source: two to six sentences, without looking.

## Key points

- The same study time gives more when it is spread out than when it is done in one block. [[how-learning-works#Spacing]]
- The best gap depends on how long the material has to last: a larger share of a short delay, a smaller share of a long one. [[how-learning-works#Spacing]]

## Self-test

> [!question]- You learned a topic today and the exam is in 30 days. Roughly when should you review it, and why not tonight?
> Come back after a day or two and let the gap grow after each successful recall, keeping it short compared with the time left. Reviewing the same evening is close to massed study, which the synthesis of 317 experiments found weaker than spaced study. [[how-learning-works#Spacing]]

> [!question]- How does the best gap between two study sessions change with the time until the test?
> As a share of the delay it shrinks: about 20 to 40 percent when the test is a week away, and about 5 to 10 percent when it is a year away. [[how-learning-works#Spacing]]
