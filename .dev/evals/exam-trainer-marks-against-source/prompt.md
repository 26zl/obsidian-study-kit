I am practising with the exam trainer in DEMO101. The question was number 1 in the question bank: a student rereads a chapter four times and feels well prepared, another reads it once and tests themselves three times. Who is likely to remember more a week later, and why is the first student's confidence misleading?

My answer, closed book, about 8 minutes:

The student who tested themselves remembers more a week later. Rereading makes the text feel familiar and that feeling is misleading. In the study the testing group was already clearly ahead after five minutes and stayed ahead after a week, so testing is simply better at every point.

Confidence: high. Please mark it.
